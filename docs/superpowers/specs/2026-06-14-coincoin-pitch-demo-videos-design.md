# coincoin — pitch + demo videos (design / production bible)

> Two videos for the **Arbitrum Open House London** submission (Robinhood Chain track): a **pitch** (~80s) and a **demo** (~95s), in English. Produced with **Remotion** (code → MP4), narration via **fish.audio** TTS, demo **recreated in-engine** with the real on-chain data from the 2026-06-14 redeploy.

## Goals
- **Pitch:** make a judge *get it* in 80s — problem, solution, why it's novel, proof. Comic/arcade duck brand.
- **Demo:** show it *actually working* end-to-end with real values (victim `0xfa14…3368`, vault 0→800, real tx hashes).

## Approach (decided)
- **Medium:** Remotion project in `video/` (isolated from `site/`). Renders `pitch.mp4` + `demo.mp4`, 1920×1080 @ 30fps.
- **Voice:** narration generated by the user on [fish.audio](https://fish.audio) from the tagged scripts below (one MP3 per scene), dropped into `video/public/audio/{pitch,demo}/NN.mp3`.
- **Audio sync:** each scene auto-sizes to its clip's duration via `@remotion/media-utils` in `calculateMetadata`; missing audio → a sensible default duration, so the video previews/renders fine **before** the voices exist and re-syncs once they're added.
- **Demo visuals:** recreated in-engine (animated terminals + dashboard cards) — not a live screen capture.

## Brand (from `docs/brand/BRAND.md`)
- Colors: bg `#0B1B3A`→`#078BEB`, surface `#032B73`, card `#0351A6`, primary/duck `#F5D90A`, danger `#FF3B3B`, success `#27C93F`, info `#3EB5F3`, outline `#040607`, text `#F3F5F3`.
- Fonts: **Bungee** (display, UPPERCASE), **IBM Plex Sans** (body), tabular-nums for numbers.
- Mascot states: **calm**=monitoring (blue glow), **alert**=threat (red glow + yellow), **hero**=evacuated (green glow). Comic bevel shadows (`8px 8px 0 #040607`), brick tiles, BOOM/POW stamps.
- Reused assets (copied into `video/public/img/`): `duck-{calm,alert,hero}.png`, `hero-{thief,chest,sparks}.png`, `{boom,pow}-stamp.png`, `bg-brick-tile.png`, `icons/{eoa,keeper,vault,alerts}.png`, `eip-7702-flip.png`, `response-window.png`, `aave-fork-test.png`, `coincoin-illustration.png`.

## Narration scripts (paste into fish.audio, 1 generation = 1 MP3)
Recommended voice: a composed, confident **US male** (e.g. "Adrian"); tech-trailer energy, punch on the tagged words. Save each as `video/public/audio/pitch/NN.mp3` / `video/public/audio/demo/NN.mp3`.

**PITCH**
- `01` — In 2025, wallet drainers stole [emphasis] eighty-three point eight five million dollars — from over a hundred thousand people. [long pause] Not one of them got it back.
- `02` — Every exploit leaves a window. From four minutes [long pause] to five days. The tools you know block a bad signature — but after a compromise? [long pause] Nothing. And for the funds you deposited in DeFi — nothing at all.
- `03` — Meet coincoin. [emphasis] A self-custodial onchain firewall. It hears the threat, and it moves your money to safety — [emphasis] on its own. The same EIP-7702 trick attackers use to drain wallets? [chuckle] We turned it into a guard dog.
- `04` — One delegation. When a real onchain threat fires, coincoin exits your DeFi positions, then sweeps every token to a vault only [emphasis] you control. Funds at rest, and funds in DeFi — the blind spot nobody else covered. Your keys never leave your hands.
- `05` — This isn't a mockup. It's live on Robinhood Chain. Ninety tests. A real Aave V3 exit, fork-verified against mainnet. End-to-end rescue, proven onchain — eight hundred dollars pulled to safety in one move.
- `06` — coincoin. The onchain firewall that shouts [emphasis] coin coin — and saves your funds before you even wake up.

**DEMO**
- `01` — Here's our user. Five hundred dollars sitting in their wallet, three hundred more deposited in Aave. One command delegates their account to coincoin — vault locked, keeper armed.
- `02` — The watcher goes live, reading real logs on Robinhood Chain. [long pause] Calm. Waiting.
- `03` — Then — the attacker strikes. A thousand dollars, drained from the protocol. A real exploit. A real onchain log.
- `04` — coincoin hears it instantly. [emphasis] Coin coin! It exits the Aave position, then evacuates everything to the user's vault. Two transactions — no human in the loop.
- `05` — The result, onchain: the wallet, empty. The vault — [emphasis] eight hundred dollars, safe. And the firewall? A malicious approval request — [emphasis] reverted on the spot.
- `06` — When it's over, one transaction removes the delegation. [long pause] A hundred percent non-custodial. Your funds, your keys — guarded by a duck.

## Deliverables / run
- `cd video && pnpm install`
- Preview: `pnpm dev` (Remotion Studio) · Render: `pnpm render` (→ `out/pitch.mp4`, `out/demo.mp4`).
- Drop the fish.audio MP3s in `public/audio/**` then re-render to get the voiced cut. Optional bg music: `public/audio/music.mp3` (kept low; none shipped — no copyrighted track committed).
